So, second entry.
I was talking about the hogwarts game in the last entry
I bought the game on steam about two days ago?
it was 5.99(usd)

I'd always seen the game on youtube but i never bothered to try it out
so when i saw it was on sale?, i thought why not

So i decided to buy it, downloaded it

Let's get this out the way, the game is very beautiful i'll give it that
but or an RPG it's really plain i'll explain


So when i was buying this game i was expecting a huge open worl with a nice story okay
they crushed the open world aspect, 10/10
but the story?

really bad
not bad in a writing sense
but like your actions and choices don't mean shit
imagine being given two options, like that one quest that you had to help a classmate steal a feather from professor sharp or whatever his name is
i declined helping the classmate
A proper RPG would make that classmate remember i didn't help
so imagine i need his help next time and he declines why?
cause i didn't help him several quests ago
there is no depth
your choices matter little
and your character really has no personality
the game just gives us all the same mc
but i'd like it if my mc had personality
what i mean is
imagine i decide to be a cold ruthless bastard
the game should respond to me the same way
other characters don't really want to help or associate with me
or if i wanted to be a sweet and loving mc other characters should be drawn to me naturally
you understand what i'm getting at?

Asides all these i do like the combat, really nice how it let's you create new combo's yourself
the world is beautiful as well
just wished the game had depth

Anyways that would be all for now
until next time
